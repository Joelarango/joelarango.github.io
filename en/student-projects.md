# Student projects

This space presents projects developed by students under my academic and technical guidance. Its purpose is to recognize their authorship, document their results, and show how engineering education can become solutions with real-world applications.

---

## Featured project

### Mixed Reality Platform for PLC Operation in Academy and Industry Environment

A mixed reality platform for visualizing and interacting with PLC systems in academic and industrial contexts.

**Student authors**

- Jorge Emilio García Contreras.
- Javier Villafuerte de Gortari.

**Academic and technical advisor**

- Joel Arango-Ramirez.

**Academic outcome**

Published in the proceedings of the *12th International Conference of Control, Dynamic Systems, and Robotics (CDSR 2025)*, held in London, United Kingdom.

- [View publication and DOI 10.11159/cdsr25.143](https://doi.org/10.11159/cdsr25.143)

**Areas developed**

- Mixed reality applied to automation.
- PLC operation and visualization.
- Training interface design.
- Integration of academic and industrial environments.
