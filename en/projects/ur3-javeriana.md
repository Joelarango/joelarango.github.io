# UR3 teleoperation at Pontificia Universidad Javeriana

## Bogotá, Colombia — 2024

Implementation of a mixed reality platform for visualizing and teleoperating a Universal Robots UR3 collaborative robot during a research stay at Pontificia Universidad Javeriana.

## Objective

Make collaborative robot operation more accessible to students and non-expert users through an immersive interface for intuitively observing, understanding, and executing motions.

## Technologies

- Meta Quest 3.
- Unity and C#.
- Universal Robots UR3.
- Digital twin.
- Python and network communication.

## Results

- Integration of the virtual and physical robot.
- Mixed reality teleoperation demonstration.
- Application in an international academic context.
- Experimental basis for the paper presented at CDSR 2024.
