# UR5 teleoperation with Meta Quest 3

## Białystok University of Technology, Poland — 2025

International demonstration of a mixed reality platform developed to teleoperate a Universal Robots UR5 using Meta Quest 3 and a digital twin created in Unity.

## Objective

Validate that an immersive interface and real-time communication architecture can adapt to a robot and infrastructure located at another institution.

## Technologies

- Meta Quest 3.
- Unity and Meta XR SDK.
- Universal Robots UR5.
- Digital twin.
- Python, MQTT, and UDP.
- RoboDK.

## Results

- Adaptation of the system to a UR5 robot.
- Teleoperation through a mixed reality interface.
- Validation of the digital twin and bidirectional communication.
- Presentation of the development in an international academic environment.
