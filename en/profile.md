# Profile and experience

## Cover letter

I am a **mechatronics engineer, lecturer, and PhD candidate in Engineering Sciences**, with experience developing and integrating solutions that connect artificial intelligence, robotics, automation, extended reality, embedded systems, and web platforms.

My work covers complete projects: from sensors, microcontrollers, and physical robots to communications, databases, web applications, digital twins, and immersive interfaces. This comprehensive perspective allows me to design cyber-physical systems in which hardware, software, and user experience operate as one solution.

I currently serve as **Head of Special Projects in the Department of Engineering Studies for Innovation** at Universidad Iberoamericana Mexico City. I also conduct doctoral research on human–robot interaction, multi-agent systems, and the cumulative transfer of physical skills between humans and heterogeneous robots.

## Value proposition

I combine engineering, research, technology development, and teaching to:

- Transform real needs into functional prototypes and systems.
- Integrate physical devices with applications, servers, databases, and cloud services.
- Design intuitive experiences for operating, supervising, and learning with robotic systems.
- Coordinate multidisciplinary automation, AI, XR, and IoT projects.
- Guide students from problem definition through integration, testing, and prototype documentation.
- Combine electronics, control, telecommunications, and software to build robust, reproducible solutions.
